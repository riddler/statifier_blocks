### Changed

- The Map draws a key of its marks beside the drawing, at its top right: the start dot, the end rings, solid, dashed and dotted arrows, the fork, the join, the hourglass and the clock, each with the drawing's own glyph and one line saying what it means, and only the marks the drawing uses. `StatifierBlocks.Map.graph/2` answers it as a new top-level `legend` list, one `%{"mark" => mark, "line" => line, "lines" => lines}` per mark, which the Map hook draws; the hook widens the SVG to hold it. A host drawing the graph itself finds the key there.
- `StatifierBlocks.Map.Info.idle/4` answers the key's lines as a new last fact, `{"Key", lines}`, since the map is hidden from assistive technology, and its `explanation`, the paragraph on how to read the map, is shorter: what each mark means moves to the key, and the paragraph points at it. `StatifierBlocks.Editor.MapRegions.description_region/1` shows the fact with a map beside it and leaves it out with `map={false}`, as it leaves out the paragraph. A host that matches the idle description's facts or its explanation text reads the new values.
